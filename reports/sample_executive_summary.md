# Executive Summary  

## Toolkit modules

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| AI System Inventory | Records university AI systems, owners, deployment status, and risk tier |
| Current Deployment Risk Monitor | Audits live systems such as AI detectors, chatbots, VLE analytics, and career AI |
| Future Deployment Readiness | Assesses AI-assisted marking, early-alert prediction, automated scoring, and admissions-support tools |
| Gender AI Proficiency Gap Monitor | Tracks unequal AI access, confidence, usage, and training |
| Governance Evidence Pack Validator | Checks whether DPIA, equality review, appeals, monitoring, and vendor evidence exist |
| Risk Scoring Engine | Converts evidence and control maturity into residual risk and deployment recommendation |

## Demonstration case studies

### Case Study 1: AI detection tool stress test

The synthetic AI detection dataset demonstrates how a university could audit an AI plagiarism or AI-writing detector for:

- False positive rate by gender
- False negative rate by gender
- Detection flag rate by gender
- Human review rate by gender
- Appeal success rate by gender
- Misconduct case escalation rate by gender

This is a synthetic stress test only. It does not prove real-world gender harm.

### Case Study 2: OULAD VLE analytics audit

The OULAD workflow demonstrates how learning analytics data can be prepared for:

- Representation analysis
- VLE engagement fairness checks
- Proxy-bias detection
- Outcome gap monitoring
- Early-alert risk review

OULAD must be downloaded separately and should not be committed to GitHub as raw third-party data unless licence and file-size considerations are checked.

## Deployment decision outcomes

| Recommendation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Approve | Controls are strong and residual risk is low |
| Approve with monitoring | Risk is manageable but requires ongoing review |
| Pilot only | Evidence is incomplete, so the system should not scale yet |
| Pause deployment | Material fairness or governance gaps must be resolved |
| Reject / prohibit | System creates unacceptable risk or lacks essential safeguards |
